**Keyboard and mouse update** for the native PC port of **PROTOTYPE** (PS4 *PROTOTYPE BIOHAZARD BUNDLE*, CUSA06545).

**No game files are included.** You need your own legally obtained, decrypted dump of CUSA06545 (01.00). Your game folder is never modified.

### Download and play
1. Download **PROTOTYPE-PC-Port-v0.5.0.zip** below and unpack it anywhere (or over 0.4.0: your saves in `user\` are kept).
2. Start **Prototype.exe**, choose your game folder, press **PLAY**.

### New in 0.5.0
- **A PC layout for keyboard and mouse**, made for PROTOTYPE's actions:

  | Action | Keys | Action | Keys |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Move | W A S D | Power wheel (hold) | Tab |
  | Camera | Mouse | Disguise menu (hold), vehicle weapon | F |
  | Jump | Space | Offensive / vision / defensive power | 1 / 2 / 3 |
  | Sprint, glide (hold) | Left Shift | Disguise | 4 or Z |
  | Attack | Left click | Reset camera | Middle click or V |
  | Special attack, consume | Right click | Pause | Esc |
  | Grab, throw | E | Web of Intrigue | J |
  | Target lock, aim (hold) | Q | Menus | Arrows, Enter, Backspace |

- **CONTROLS page in the launcher**: two keys per action (any key, mouse button or wheel), mouse look and sensitivity.
- **Keyboard and mouse button icons in game**: the hints and tutorials show your keys (`[E]`, `[SPACE]`, mouse pictures) instead of the PS4 buttons. *Button icons in game* on the CONTROLS page: Keyboard & mouse, PlayStation, or Automatic (what you played with last). Made at start from your own game files into `out\prompts`; the game folder is not changed.
- **F10** writes a hang report to `last_run.log` (for problem reports).

### Known issues
- The pause menu can rarely stay on its loading screen: press **F10** while it hangs and send `last_run.log`
- If you change the controller layout in the game's own options, keys and icons do not follow it
- PROTOTYPE 2 (bundle menu) does not start yet
- Tested on AMD Radeon only — **NVIDIA and Intel are untested**, please report how they run
- FSR 4.1 is hidden: AMD's Windows driver lacks a Vulkan feature it needs

### Reporting problems
Open an issue with your GPU, driver and `last_run.log` from the port folder.
