**Graphics modding and bigger, sharper shadows.**
- You can now replace PROTOTYPE's shaders with your own, and an in-game browser shows which shader draws what.
- PROTOTYPE gets new shadow options. They are **very experimental**.
- The black river water is fixed.

**No game files are included.** You need your own legally obtained, decrypted dump of CUSA06545 (01.00): the folder with `eboot.bin`, `prototype2_ps4f.elf`, `sce_sys`, `sce_module`, `p1` and `p2`. Your game folder is never modified.

### Download and play
1. Download **PROTOTYPE-PC-Port-v0.7.0.zip** below and unpack it anywhere. You can also unpack it over an earlier version: your saves in `user\` are kept.
2. Start **Prototype.exe** and choose your game folder.
3. Pick **PROTOTYPE** or **PROTOTYPE 2** at the top, then press **PLAY**.

### New in 0.7.0

#### ⚠️ Shadow options (PROTOTYPE): very experimental
These are on the launcher's **GAME** page under **SHADOWS · VERY EXPERIMENTAL**, and are off by default. They have only been tried briefly on one PC.

- **Shadow resolution: 4096.** The sun's shadow map is 4096 × 4096 instead of the PS4's 2048 × 2048, so every shadow has twice the detail: sharper edges and less shimmer. It uses 64 MB more video memory.
- **Shadow distance: 2×, 3× or 4×.** Shadows reach that many times as far. The same shadow detail is spread over the longer distance, so shadows are softer up close. With 4096 shadows, 2× keeps the PS4's sharpness.
- **Shadow distance: Whole city.** The game splits the sun's shadow into four bands by distance, and this sets each band separately.
  - On the PS4 the bands end at about 5, 10, 20 and 120 m. Here they end at about 8, 40, 200 and 1500 m.
  - Shadows near you keep their detail, and far buildings can still cast shadows.
  - Best with 4096.

If shadows go missing, look shifted, float off objects, flicker, or the game crashes while loading, set them back to **2048** and **PS4** and please report it with a screenshot and `last_run.log`. Like every fix in the port, these are applied in memory: the game files stay untouched.

#### Graphics modding
- **Shader mods.** Put GLSL shaders in `mods\shaders` and they replace the game's own shaders. The port compiles changed files at start; the in-game menu shows how many loaded, or the first error.
- **Shader browser.** In the port menu (**Insert** / **R3 + L2**) on the new **SHADERS** page:
  - It lists the pixel shaders of the last frame, most draws first. Known ones are named: river, depth of field, ambient occlusion, post effects and others.
  - **HIDE THIS SHADER** leaves it out of the picture, so you can see what it draws.
  - **EXPORT FOR EDITING** writes it to `mods\shaders` as editable GLSL.
- The release includes `glslangValidator` and `spirv-cross` for this. The guide is [docs/SHADER_MODS.md](https://github.com/petrishevgeorgii-debug/prototype-pc-slop/blob/main/docs/SHADER_MODS.md).
- Exports come from your own game. Shaders are never shipped with the port.

#### Fixes
- **Black river water.** The river's reflection was dropped by the GPU layer, so the water rendered black. It now shows the sky reflection as on the PS4.

#### Also
- **Water reflections** (screen-space reflections on the river) are experimental and off by default. They are on the launcher's Graphics page and in the port menu.

### Known issues
- **The shadow options are very experimental** (see above).
  - Whole city can cost frame rate.
  - Distant buildings may still cast no shadow with it, because their far-away models may not be shadow casters.
- **PROTOTYPE 2 is still experimental.** It has no keyboard and mouse button icons, FSR upscaling does not work in it, and its mouse camera goes through the right stick.
- If a loading screen never ends, press **F10** while it hangs. Then send `last_run.log` and `out\hang_sample.txt`.
- Render resolutions above 1080p are still experimental in both games.
- FSR 4.1 stays hidden: AMD's Windows driver lacks a Vulkan feature it needs.
