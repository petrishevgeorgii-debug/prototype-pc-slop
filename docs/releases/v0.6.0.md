**PROTOTYPE 2 now starts and plays** (experimental). It has its own render resolution and 60 FPS patches, and the launcher has been redesigned with a big tab for each game. Also new: the mouse turns PROTOTYPE's on-foot camera directly, as in a PC game.

**No game files are included.** You need your own legally obtained, decrypted dump of CUSA06545 (01.00): the folder with `eboot.bin`, `prototype2_ps4f.elf`, `sce_sys`, `sce_module`, `p1` and `p2`. Your game folder is never modified.

### Download and play
1. Download **PROTOTYPE-PC-Port-v0.6.0.zip** below and unpack it anywhere (or over an earlier version: your saves in `user\` are kept).
2. Start **Prototype.exe** and choose your game folder.
3. Pick **PROTOTYPE** or **PROTOTYPE 2** at the top, then press **PLAY**.

### New in 0.6.0
- **PROTOTYPE 2 (experimental).** It starts from its launcher tab or from the bundle menu, and plays at 30 FPS or 60 FPS. Getting there took these fixes:
  - **Black screen at start.** The game's PSN thread sometimes sent its "ready" signal before the main thread waited for it, so the signal was lost.
  - **Crash after the Activision logo.** The game binds one buffer of several GiB, which the GPU layer could not hold. Buffers like it are now bound in parts the GPU can take.
  - **Exploding geometry.** The GPU layer's draw-preparation threads read each draw's vertex tables before PROTOTYPE 2 had filled them. They are turned off for PROTOTYPE 2. Vertex streams that point to old data are now also read as empty.
  - **101 system functions** PROTOTYPE 2 uses that PROTOTYPE does not, such as PSN storage, scores, matchmaking, messages, dialogs and date parsing. Online features answer as offline.
- **PROTOTYPE 2 patches,** in a file of their own (`patches/Prototype2-Orbis.xml`):
  - **Render resolution** from 1280 × 720 up to 3840 × 2160. Above 1080p the game gets more video memory for its render targets.
  - **60 FPS** in place of the game's 30 FPS cap.
  - As with PROTOTYPE, the patches are applied in memory and the game files stay untouched.
- **Redesigned launcher.**
  - Two big game tabs across the top, each in its own look: PROTOTYPE in black and red, PROTOTYPE 2 in steel and blue. Each tab shows its half of the bundle's key art.
  - The new **GAME** page holds the selected game's own settings: render resolution, frame rate, and PROTOTYPE's tendril fix. Each game keeps its own, and your earlier settings stay with PROTOTYPE.
  - Graphics, display and controls are shared by both games.
  - Under PLAY, a short line shows what the game will start with, and one click opens the page to change it.
- **PC-style mouse look in PROTOTYPE.** The mouse turns the on-foot camera directly, by exactly the distance you move it, with no dead zone, curve or turn-speed limit. The game's invert options and pitch limits still apply. After you stop moving the mouse, the camera waits 1.5 s before swinging back behind Alex. Vehicles and menus keep the stick-style mouse.
- **Fewer stalls.** Each semaphore now has its own lock: before, every thread queued behind one shared lock on every signal and wait.
- **Automatic hang report.** If a compressed game file stays half-read for a minute, the hang report is written by itself, the same one **F10** writes.

### Known issues
- **PROTOTYPE 2 is experimental.**
  - It shows the PlayStation buttons in its hints: keyboard and mouse button icons are PROTOTYPE only for now. The keys themselves work.
  - FSR upscaling does not work in PROTOTYPE 2 yet.
  - The mouse turns its camera through the right stick (it feels like a stick). PROTOTYPE's direct mouse camera is not in PROTOTYPE 2 yet.
  - 4K (3840 × 2160) has not been tested in PROTOTYPE 2.
- If a loading screen never ends, press **F10** while it hangs. Then send `last_run.log` and `out\hang_sample.txt`.
- Render resolutions above 1080p are still experimental in both games.
- Testing so far: AMD Radeon and one NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Ti. Please report how other GPUs run.
- FSR 4.1 stays hidden: AMD's Windows driver lacks a Vulkan feature it needs.
