**A new launcher, and two experiments scrapped.**
- The launcher is redesigned from scratch as a Blackwatch containment terminal.
- The very experimental shadow options of 0.7.0 are gone, and so are the water reflections.

**No game files are included.** You need your own legally obtained, decrypted dump of CUSA06545 (01.00): the folder with `eboot.bin`, `prototype2_ps4f.elf`, `sce_sys`, `sce_module`, `p1` and `p2`. Your game folder is never modified.

### Download and play
1. Download **PROTOTYPE-PC-Port-v1.0.1.zip** below and unpack it anywhere. You can also unpack it over an earlier version: your saves and settings in `user\` are kept.
2. Start **Prototype.exe** and choose your game folder.
3. Pick **PROTOTYPE** or **PROTOTYPE 2** with the file tabs at the top left, then press **PLAY** (or Enter).

### New in 1.0.1

#### The launcher, redesigned
- **The look:** monospace type, CRT scanlines, hazard stripes and a dossier for each game.
- **The subject feed:** the bundle's key art from your own game folder, shown as a surveillance camera, with the odd glitch. Without a game folder it shows NO SIGNAL.
- **The subject file:** the game's name, its resolution and frame rate, a big **PLAY** button, and an integrity scan of your game folder (`[ OK ]`, `[WARN]`, `[FAIL]`).
- **The console:** the settings pages **1 GAME**, **2 GRAPHICS**, **3 DISPLAY**, **4 CONTROLS** and **5 FOLDER**.
  - Point at a setting and the info box types out what it does.
  - The GAME page also lists the shared settings the game starts with. Click one to go to its page.
- **The status line** at the bottom, with **Game folder**, **Saves**, **Backups**, **Log** and **Port folder**.
- **Keys:** 1 to 5 switch the pages and Enter starts the game.
- Every setting is where it was: key rebinding, the folder check, and Prepare, Restore original and Clean up.

#### Removed
- **The shadow options** (shadow resolution 4096, shadow distance 2× to 4× and Whole city). They were very experimental and are scrapped. PROTOTYPE's shadows are the PS4's again.
- **Water reflections** (screen-space reflections on the river). They did not look good enough. The river shows the game's own sky reflection, as on the PS4.
- The launcher's Manhattan Engine button. The engine is an early preview and is not part of the release.

### Known issues
- **PROTOTYPE 2 is still experimental.** It has no keyboard and mouse button icons, FSR upscaling does not work in it, and its mouse camera goes through the right stick.
- If a loading screen never ends, press **F10** while it hangs. Then send `last_run.log` and `out\hang_sample.txt`.
- Render resolutions above 1080p are still experimental in both games.
- FSR 4.1 stays hidden: AMD's Windows driver lacks a Vulkan feature it needs.
